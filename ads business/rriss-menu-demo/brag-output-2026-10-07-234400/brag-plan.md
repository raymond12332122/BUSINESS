# Brag plan — RRISS menu board (on-screen content)

## Planning rubric
1. **What is it?** The actual animated menu that plays full-screen on a vertical TV inside Bread & Coffee RRISS.
2. **Who watches it?** Customers in line at the counter. They glance for a few seconds, so every segment shows one clear thing: a product, its name, its price.
3. **What matters?** Seeing the whole menu, then featured products that make people order (Blueberry Boba, taro, frappé, café & pan, the combo).
4. **Real visuals:** their product photos (boba, taro, frappé, fresas, malteada, helados, crepas) and 3D renders (café, croissant, pastel, donas).
5. **Copy:** Spanish. Product names from their posts; prices are sample prices to replace with the real ones.
6. **Hook:** the full menu grid builds itself card by card in the first second.
7. **Tone:** `polished` + warm cafetería: calm, appetizing, clean.
8. **Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30s seamless loop.
9. **Palette (user):** white, gold, and a soft blue (less saturated than the last demo).

**Why 30s instead of brag's 15–25s:** the user asked for a new segment every 5 seconds (menu, boba, taro, frappé, café & pan, combo). Six 5-second segments = 30s, and it loops on the screen.

## Creative angle
A premium, light menu board. Soft sky-blue canvas, white cards, gold price badges, navy-blue type. Persistent header (RRISS logo), segment dots and a scrolling footer band tie the loop together. Each feature gets one product-specific animation.

Palette: bg `#E6EDF8`, card `#FFFFFF`, soft blue `#7A9BD8`, text blue `#23407E`, mid blue `#4E6FB5`, gold `#C9A24A`, gold light `#E2C26A`.
Type: DM Serif Display (names), Outfit (prices), JetBrains Mono (labels).

## Audio
Menu boards in stores usually play muted. The brag music bundle is missing from this install, so the render is silent.
