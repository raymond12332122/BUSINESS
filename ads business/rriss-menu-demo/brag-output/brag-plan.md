# Brag plan — RRISS menu boards (pitch demo)

## Planning rubric
1. **What is it?** A pitch demo: Bread & Coffee RRISS's menu running as animated digital menu boards ("animación de menús para pantallas").
2. **Who is it for?** The owners of Bread & Coffee RRISS (panadería, pastelería, cafetería). The video must make them picture their own counter with these screens.
3. **What does it do that matters?** Their products and prices come alive on screen: drinks page, bakery page, a combo promo, and their brand panel. It replaces static photos and blurry Facebook flyers.
4. **What is the real visual to show?** Their own product photos (taro, Blueberry Boba, frappé, fresas con crema, malteada, crepas) plus 3D renders of café, croissant, pastel and donas.
5. **Copy to use:** Spanish. Product names from their posts; demo prices clearly sample prices.
6. **Hook:** "ANIMACIÓN DE MENÚS / PARA PANTALLAS" kinetic headline, then the screens light up.
7. **Tone:** `polished` + "premium cafetería": calm confidence, crisp motion, no jokes.
8. **Format:** vertical 1080×1920 (Reels/TikTok/WhatsApp status), 22s.
9. **Palette constraint (user):** only white, blue and gold.

## Creative angle
Mirror the reference reel's structure (in-store screen wall on top, two close-up portrait screens below) but made for RRISS: cream-white canvas, deep royal blue brand panel, gold for prices, rules and the logo.

Palette: bg `#F6F3EC` (warm white), ink `#0E2A6B` (navy), accent blue `#1F4FD8`, gold `#C9A24A` / text-gold `#A9832F` on white.
Type: DM Serif Display (names, brand), Outfit (prices, UI), JetBrains Mono (labels).
