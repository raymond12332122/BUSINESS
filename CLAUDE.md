# Video style

Brag videos and Remotion videos should use the motion-graphics look of the "Holetex Motion Designer Reel 2024" reference (watched via the watch skill):

- Bold, tightly tracked kinetic type that builds word by word.
- Plain high-contrast backgrounds: cream, black, or one saturated accent color.
- Geometric motion: dot grids, rotating shapes, particle fields, circles that expand to fill the frame.
- Quick cuts with crisp timing; brief code-style labels as accents.

Match the look and motion only. Do not copy the reference's wording, logo, or branding.
